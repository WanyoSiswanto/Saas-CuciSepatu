# SNEAKERCARE LAB — Iconic Atelier Design System

> **Design Read:** Premium, friendly, and iconic shoe care service management.
> **Vibe:** Soft, clean, bright white canvas with crisp fresh water blues, sunlight yellow, and energetic citrus orange accents.
> **Forbidden:** Dark cyberpunk/hacker matrix themes, gloomy carbon/obsidian backgrounds, generic AI purple gradients, boring sterile corporate templates.

---

## 1. THE THREE DIALS (Configuration)

* **`DESIGN_VARIANCE: 8`** — Dynamic, playful yet structured layout. Soft floating cards, rounded geometry, vibrant badge pops.
* **`MOTION_INTENSITY: 5`** — Smooth, gentle spring transitions, tactile button presses, subtle hover lifts.
* **`VISUAL_DENSITY: 5`** — Balanced whitespace, breathable forms, clear visual hierarchy for fast counter operations.

---

## 2. COLOR PALETTE: FRESH WATER, SUNLIGHT & CITRUS

### A. The Clean Base (Canvas)
* **Background Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright White)
* **Background Soft Surface:** `#F8FAFC` (Soft Slate / Porcelain White)
* **Card & Section Surface:** `#FFFFFF` with subtle border `#E2E8F0` / `#F1F5F9` and soft shadow `shadow-[0_4px_20px_-4px_rgba(0,0,0,0.05)]`
* **Text Primary:** `#0F172A` (Deep Slate / Ink Black — sharp legibility)
* **Text Muted:** `#64748B` (Neutral Slate)
* **Text Soft:** `#94A3B8` (Light Slate)

### B. The Iconic Blue (Fresh Water & Cleansing)
* **Primary Brand Blue:** `#2563EB` (Royal Blue) & `#0284C7` (Fresh Aqua Blue)
* **Light Blue Tint (Badges & Surfaces):** `#EFF6FF` & `#E0F2FE`
* **Border Blue Accent:** `#BAE6FD` & `#BFDBFE`
* **Use:** Main call to action, active navigation, water/cleaning service highlights, primary icons.

### C. The Sunlight Yellow (Warmth, Attention & Quality)
* **Sunlight Yellow:** `#F59E0B` (Warm Amber Gold) & `#EAB308` (Bright Sun Yellow)
* **Yellow Tint Surface:** `#FEFCE8` & `#FEF3C7`
* **Border Yellow Accent:** `#FDE68A`
* **Use:** Express indicators, pending antrean, quality check tags, ratings, important notes.

### D. The Citrus Orange (Action, Energy & Completion)
* **Citrus Orange:** `#F97316` (Vibrant Orange) & `#EA580C` (Deep Tangerine)
* **Orange Tint Surface:** `#FFF7ED` & `#FFEDD5`
* **Border Orange Accent:** `#FED7AA`
* **Use:** "+ Input Order" primary callouts, deadline alerts, Before condition badges, energetic highlights.

### E. Fresh Clean Green (Approved & Ready)
* **Clean Mint / Emerald:** `#10B981` & `#059669` (For "Siap Diambil", Lunas, and Lolos QC).

---

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY & TEXTURE

* **Font Primary (UI & Headings):** `Plus Jakarta Sans` — approachable, friendly, human, crisp.
* **Font Mono (Codes & Currency):** `JetBrains Mono` — neat alignment for receipt numbers (`CS-2609-001`), prices, and dates.
* **Border Radii:** Rounded and soft (`rounded-2xl` for cards, `rounded-xl` for inputs/buttons, `rounded-full` for badges).
* **Dividers:** Soft dashed lines `border-dashed border-slate-200` reflecting laundry tickets and garment stitching.

---

## 4. DESIGN RULES & INTEGRITY

1. **No Dark Tech Gloom:** Ensure pages are illuminated, crisp, and clean. No black or dark-gray screens.
2. **Iconic Color Balance:**
   - 70% Clean White / Soft Porcelain surfaces
   - 20% Fresh Blue navigation, headings, and primary elements
   - 10% Energetic Yellow & Orange chips, badges, and focal points
3. **Tactile Delight:** Subtle button elevations, clean hover cards with slight scale/border glow, and clear status feedback.
